SEC. 03 — PROCESS

How a job runs

The same four stages, whether it's a rooftop install or a kitchen remodel.

01

Walkthrough & scope

We look at the site in person, talk through what you need, and put the scope of work in writing before anything is priced.

02

Estimate & permits

You get a itemized estimate. If the job needs permits or plan review, we file and track them — that's on us, not you.

03

Build

Our crew works the schedule we agreed on. You get a single point of contact for the life of the job, not a rotating cast.

04

Walkthrough & close-out

Final walkthrough, punch list cleared, and documentation handed over — inspections, warranties, and as-builts included.

SEC. 05 — ABOUT

Family-run, Folsom-based

Magness & Sons General Contracting has operated out of Folsom, Pennsylvania since 1996, working commercial, office, and residential jobs across Delaware County and the wider Philadelphia metro area.

The company is led by President Thomas W. Magness, and keeps a core crew of roughly 25 tradespeople rather than scaling through subcontractors — which is part of why clients get the same point of contact from the first walkthrough to the final punch list.

If you're checking us out before hiring, verify current licensing directly with your local licensing authority — standard practice for any contractor, us included.
